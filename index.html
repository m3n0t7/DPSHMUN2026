About the Conference

At Delhi Public School Hyderabad, we believe that today's students are tomorrow's leaders, and that meaningful dialogue is the foundation of positive global change. DPSHMUN continues to provide a platform where young minds can challenge perspectives, strengthen their voices, and develop the confidence to engage with the world's most pressing issues.

We are delighted to announce DPSHMUN 2026, to be held on 7th and 8th August 2026. This year's conference will bring together aspiring delegates for two days of rigorous debate, collaborative problem-solving, and immersive diplomacy.Join us for two days of engaging committee sessions, thoughtful deliberation, and inspiring leadership.

Letter from the Secretary General

Dear Delegates,

As the Secretary General, I take immense pride in welcoming you to DPSH MUN 2026.

This year, committees may remain the same, but our attempt is to embody the true spirit of deliberation and the essence of understanding the actual workings of the United Nations lives higher than ever.

It is an effort to make you fall in love with the art of reading between the lines, understanding world dynamics, and appreciating the realities behind every agenda.

It is an attempt not to reduce MUN to just suits, gadgets, parties and awards, but to celebrate the spirit of deliberation.

To all newcomers stepping into the world of MUNs , best wishes! Starting something new takes courage, and we're all here to ensure your journey is unforgettable.

On behalf of my Secretariat, I look forward to welcoming you to our conference.

Warm Regards,
Pritha Kesharwani
